What we store

NeuroMCQ stores your profile (name, a masked phone number such as 98****1234, and a one-way SHA-256 hash of the phone number used as an identifier) and your learning activity (answers, timings, scores, notes, bookmarks) on your own device in your browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to a server unless the site owner has enabled optional sync.

Optional sync

If the owner enables sync, your user file may be stored in the owner's GitHub repository, Firebase or Supabase project. Phone hashes of 10-digit numbers can be guessed by brute force, so the owner must keep that storage private. Minors should use the app only with a parent or guardian's consent.

Your rights

You can download all your data (Settings → Download my data), import it on another device, or permanently delete it (Settings → Delete all my data).

Advertising

If ads are enabled, Google AdSense (web) or Google AdMob (Android app) may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads, subject to your consent choice. Ads are never shown during questions, tests, battles or rapid-fire. See how Google uses data.

Live battles

When you join a live room, your display name, answers and scores are shared with the other players in that room through the owner's configured real-time service.
